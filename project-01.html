PROJECT 01 · AI / AUTOMATION

IYKFixitMobile AI Customer Service Assistant

An AI customer-service workflow prototype designed to help small businesses answer customer questions, qualify leads and escalate complex enquiries to a human agent.

The Business Problem

Small businesses often receive repeated questions about products, services, pricing, availability and repairs. Handling these enquiries manually can consume time and cause potential customers to be missed.

The Proposed Solution

I designed an AI customer-service workflow that uses a structured business knowledge base to provide consistent responses while identifying buying intent and knowing when to hand the conversation over to a human.

How It Works

Customer → Chat Channel → AI Assistant → Business Knowledge Base → Response → Lead Qualification / Human Handoff

Core Capabilities

  • Answer frequently asked customer questions
  • Use business information as a knowledge source
  • Qualify potential customers and buying enquiries
  • Identify situations requiring human assistance
  • Avoid inventing prices, stock or business information
  • Protect sensitive customer information

Example Use Cases

  • Customer asking about available smartphones
  • Customer asking about phone repairs
  • Customer requesting a price that is not in the knowledge base
  • Customer ready to purchase
  • Customer requesting to speak with a human

Project Approach

The project focuses on practical AI automation rather than simply generating generic chatbot responses. The assistant is designed to follow business rules, use trusted information and escalate situations where an automated response may not be appropriate.

Project Status

Prototype / workflow design. The project demonstrates the architecture, business rules, knowledge-base structure and conversational logic for a future production implementation.
